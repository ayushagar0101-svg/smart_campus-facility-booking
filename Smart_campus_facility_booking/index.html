About CampusBook

Built to replace the sign-up sheet on the noticeboard

CampusBook centralises every bookable space on campus — classrooms, labs, courts and study rooms — so students and faculty can check real-time availability and reserve a slot in minutes, while facility staff manage requests from one dashboard instead of a dozen spreadsheets.
